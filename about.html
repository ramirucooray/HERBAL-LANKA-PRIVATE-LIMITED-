Our History

A Legacy of Purity

Established in the lush highlands of Sri Lanka, Herbal Lanka Private Limited was born from a passion for authentic tea. We believe that the perfect cup of tea starts with the soil. That's why we partner with small-holder farmers who treat their land with respect, avoiding harmful chemicals and embracing traditional farming methods.

Our teas are not just beverages; they are a bridge to a healthier, more mindful lifestyle. From the mist-covered mountains to your cup, we ensure every step maintains the integrity of the leaf.
